# Benchmark: ESP32-C3 on the left edge, USB-C at its bottom

Source: the breadboard fence's ESP32 variant (`fence-esp32`, 4 layers, 76.2 × 34 mm plus the overhanging antenna), generated by `fence.toit --esp32` in `~/programming/breadboard-enhancer/hw` (see [../README.md](../README.md)). In this copy the parts that make up the USB/ESP corner are **not** fixed. The rest of the board is fixed where the designer put it (`layout.json`).

## Task

Place (and route) the free parts so that:

1. **The ESP32-C3-MINI-1 (U3) sits on the left edge:** flush with it, in the top-left corner, with its **antenna sticking out beyond the top edge**.
   - This is Espressif's preferred module placement: PCB antenna outside the base board, and its left-hand feed point at the board edge.
   - The antenna area is the footprint's keepout zone (local y −8.3…−2.9). It must lie entirely outside the board outline, and the module's body inside it.
2. **The USB-C receptacle (J4) sits at the bottom of the ESP32:** on the same left edge, directly below the module, opening outwards (to the left).
   - Only the module's decoupling capacitors (C5, C6) go between them. The gap should be at most a few millimetres.
3. **The 3.3 V regulator (U2, AMS1117) sits between the two:** right next to the USB connector's VBUS pins and close to the module's 3V3 pin (pin 3, on its left column).
   - Its input and output capacitors (C3, C4) sit at its pins.
4. **The board stays complete and DRC-clean on copper.**

Free parts: U3, J4, U2, C3, C4, C5, C6. Everything else is fixed.

The current board (`../v4-4layer/fence-esp32/`) has since moved on: the board is 31 mm tall, J4 sits as low as the row header allows (centre y 22.9) with RN1 moved right of it, and the CC network RN9 fills the gap between C5/C6 and J4. This benchmark keeps the v2 input.

## What makes it hard for pcb-maker (2026-09-25)

- **No edge, relative or proximity constraints** (`docs/placer.md`: "No constraint language yet"). Nothing tells the placer that U3 belongs on an edge, J4 below it, or U2 between them. Wire length alone pulls them towards the middle.
- **Overhang:** a footprint whose body leaves the outline is treated as fixed by default (`on_edge` in `board_placer.rs`). A placer that may put a part *partly outside* the board has to reason about which part of the footprint may overhang (here: exactly the antenna keepout).
- **Keepouts:** keepout zones inside footprints are not honoured by the router (issue 1 in `../README.md`).

`constraints.json` sketches how the intent could be stated:
- edge constraints with overhang;
- "J4 below U3";
- "near" limits for the regulator and the decoupling capacitors.

## Passing

| Criterion | Pass |
|---|---|
| U3 | touches the left edge; its antenna keepout lies outside the outline; its body lies inside |
| J4 | on the left edge, below U3, gap ≤ 3 mm plus C5/C6 |
| Distances | U2 within 10 mm of J4 and 12 mm of U3; C3/C4 within 4 mm of U2's pins; C5/C6 within 6 mm of U3 pin 3 |
| Copper | KiCad reports no unconnected items and no copper DRC errors |
